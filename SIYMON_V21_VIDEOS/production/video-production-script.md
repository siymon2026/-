# Video production script / shot plan

The timings below describe the final 14.10-second edits. Each contains five 2.50-second still-image shots with a 12-frame (0.40 s) dissolve between shots. A restrained 30 fps digital push-in is applied to each shot. There is no audio track and no added copy. UI text/logos are visible only where they are already part of supplied screenshots.

## 01 — Discover SIYMON

1. **0:00–0:02.50 — Product reveal:** dark studio still; genuine customer discovery screen composited into the blank phone display.
2. **0:02.50–0:05.40 — Restaurant discovery:** Moroccan-kitchen still with the authentic customer-facing restaurant list shown on a restrained device card.
3. **0:05.40–0:08.30 — Customer touchpoint:** warm home still and customer discovery UI.
4. **0:08.30–0:11.20 — Delivery connection:** blue-hour rider still paired with the supplied driver route/tracking screen.
5. **0:11.20–0:14.10 — Brand close:** original green SIYMON app icon alone on a dark green/black field.

## 02 — Customer Experience

1. **0:00–0:02.50 — At home:** warm Moroccan-home still.
2. **0:02.50–0:05.40 — Browse:** actual SIYMON customer discovery interface in a phone surround over the home scene.
3. **0:05.40–0:08.30 — Choose a restaurant:** actual customer-facing restaurant listing over the kitchen still; this is not a checkout screen.
4. **0:08.30–0:11.20 — Order progress:** supplied customer notification/order-status screen over the home still.
5. **0:11.20–0:14.10 — Brand close:** original SIYMON app icon on the dark finish.

## 03 — SIYMON Stores

1. **0:00–0:02.50 — Kitchen atmosphere:** generated Moroccan-kitchen still.

4. **0:08.30–0:11.20 — Dispatch context:** rider environment and genuine customer discovery interface, expressing the ecosystem link (not a recorded handoff).

## 04 — SIYMON Driver & Smart Delivery

1. **0:00–0:02.50 — Rider preparation:** blue-hour Moroccan urban street and rider still.
2. **0:02.50–0:05.40 — Driver operations:** actual supplied driver dashboard screen.
3. **0:05.40–0:08.30 — Driver account:** actual supplied driver profile/settings screen.
4. **0:08.30–0:11.20 — Route tracking:** actual driver order/tracking view with its embedded map.
5. **0:11.20–0:14.10 — Brand close:** original SIYMON app icon on the dark finish.

The transitions overlap the shot intervals; all four renders have a total measured duration of 14.10 seconds. The still-image scenes use camera-motion effects only, not fabricated footage or AI video interpolation.
